# Frontend Design

Guidance for creating distinctive, production-grade visual designs that avoid generic "AI slop" or cookie-cutter templates.

## Core Process

1. **Define the Context**: Identify the concrete subject matter, target audience, and primary job of the interface. Anchor visual decisions in the industry, material reality, and character of the domain.
2. **Commit to a Tone**: Choose a clear aesthetic posture (e.g., editorial, technical, warm, minimalist, utility-dense) appropriate to the product.
3. **The Design Plan (Before Writing Code)**:
   - Outline 4-6 named hex colors fitting the brief.
   - Establish clear typography hierarchy and weights.
   - Design layout and grid structures intentionally.
   - Choose one deliberate aesthetic focal point.
4. **Anti-Default Self-Critique**: Review the design plan to ensure it avoids cliché tropes (predictable purple gradients, identical rounded cards with soft gray shadows, tracked-out ALL CAPS everywhere).
5. **Quality Floor**: Ensure mobile responsiveness, visual accessibility, proper keyboard focus, and appropriate motion/transitions.

## Avoided Patterns
- The SaaS Card Kit: Slicing every piece of content into identical rounded cards with identical borders and soft shadows.
- Predictable Palettes: Default dark slates with neon gradients or generic corporate blues.
- Useless Motion: Fading up every section on scroll or adding heavy hover interactions to every element.
- Ambiguous Copy: Vague action verbs or placeholder copy. Keep action labels active, clear, and direct.
